qb-assistant

qb-assistant is a private, household assistant. It runs on a computer owned by the household it serves and helps its owners keep track of their own email, calendar and contacts: it files messages from known senders into a private notes folder so the owners can ask questions about them later.

It is not a public service. It is used only by the members of one household, with their own accounts, and it is not offered to anyone else.
